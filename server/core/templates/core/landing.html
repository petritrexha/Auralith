Learn what your AI just built, while it builds it.

Businesses are handing their code to AI agents. LearnLoop is the agent that makes sure their people still understand it — and shows managers where the gaps are.

1 · Your agent codes

Claude Code edits files as usual. LearnLoop quietly collects the diffs (secrets scrubbed on your machine).

2 · LearnLoop decides

A teaching agent checks what you already know and picks what's worth explaining — usually 0–2 things.

3 · You get a card

A short "why it's here, in your code" card appears in the terminal and in your feed. Managers see team-wide comprehension.
